# Macha Experimental Track II: InZOI

**Date:** 2026-08-16  
**Project:** [Macha](../README.md) — an open, engine-agnostic standard skeleton for game NPCs  
**Status:** Announced

## What we are announcing

The Macha team is launching a second experimental track in **InZOI**. We will explore how Macha's NPC cognitive skeleton can be tested in a modern life-simulation environment where characters already have daily routines, social relationships, emotions, and long-term lives.

This is an independent research effort. Macha is not affiliated with Krafton or the InZOI development team.

## Why InZOI

Life simulation is one of the hardest tests for an NPC standard. Characters in this genre are expected to:

- live beyond the player;
- maintain relationships and reputations;
- react to time, space, events, and social context;
- remain themselves while still being changeable by the player.

InZOI therefore gives us a strong environment for asking the question at the center of Macha:

> Can an NPC be both a stable self and a responsive, world-coupled other?

## Scope and compliance

- All experiments will respect Krafton's terms of service, copyright, and any applicable modding or API policies.
- We will not distribute game assets or unauthorized modifications.
- If no authorized integration path exists at a given stage, we will keep this track at the observation and design-mapping level until one becomes available.

## Collaboration

We welcome discussion with InZOI community members, modders, life-simulation researchers, and game AI developers. If you are working on similar experiments, or if you have relevant public documentation, we would be glad to exchange notes.
